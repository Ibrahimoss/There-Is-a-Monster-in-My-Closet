# Attribution

## Audio

**Weather** — trimmed and converted to mono OGG for the web build, from the
Sonniss GDC Game Audio Bundles (royalty-free for commercial and non-commercial
use):

- `assets/audio/weather/rain_loop.ogg` — Double Trouble Audio, "Rain and
  Thunder" (GDC 2017 bundle), from *RATH - Rain Hard Loop 08*.
- `assets/audio/weather/thunder_close.ogg`, `thunder_far.ogg` — Pole Position,
  "Thunder & Rain" (GDC 2019 bundle), from *thunderstorm summer morning distant
  rumble sparse approaching 2*.

**The scream at the window** — same bundles, same licence. Pitched down, layered
and given a tail with ffmpeg:

- `assets/audio/monster/monster_scream.ogg` — SmartSoundFX, "Monsters" (GDC 2020
  bundle), from *BEAST 01 Attack 08*, under Phase 4 Libraries, "Monster Roars"
  (GDC 2020 bundle), from *MR - Banshee Damage VO 07*.

**Music**

- `assets/audio/music/run_for_your_life.mp3` — "Run For Your Life", Apeirophobia
  original soundtrack. Supplied by the team; **clear the licence before any
  public release.**

**Sound effects**

- Doors, steps, impacts, cloth — Kenney (CC0).
- Creaking door — universfield (Pixabay).
- Crickets — alex_jauk (Pixabay).
- Air conditioner — flutie8211 (Pixabay).

Everything else in `scripts/sound_gen.gd` is generated at runtime: the drip,
toothbrush, tap, flush, drawer, heartbeat, wind, and the monster's breath,
steps, roar and glitch.
